# ERPSYS — System Specification

**Status:** Draft for review (compiled from interviews, 2026-08-21)
**Scope:** Full-stack system spec: roles, flows, data model, API, integrations.
**Companion docs:** `docs/readme.md` (original request), `design.md` (UI/motion spec — applies to all surfaces).

---

## 1. Overview

COT-SBO Enrollment Requirements Processing System (ERPSYS) digitalizes the
6-step "me card" enrollment-requirements process at the College of Technology
(COT), Bukidnon State University. Students currently queue at the SBO office in
person for each step; ERPSYS moves verification, profiling, uploads, and the
final enrollment queue online.

The process is a **per-academic-year cycle**: a student completes steps 1–5
(verified by SBO officers with digital "stamps"), then joins a **department
enrollment queue** where a faculty enrollment adviser performs final
verification and marks the student as enrolled. The COR itself is generated by
a separate university system.

---

## 2. Goals & Non-Goals

### Goals
- Replace the physical stamping flow with a verifiable digital trail (who
  verified what, when).
- Reduce SBO-office crowding: profiling, uploads, and queueing happen online;
  only in-person touchpoints remain (assessment walk-in, mugshot capture).
- Give students a mobile-optimized progress dashboard with a live view of the
  6 steps.
- Give SBO officers a per-step verification queue.
- Give each department's enrollment adviser an organized FIFO queue so
  students are served one by one, not in a crowd.
- AI chatbot answers process questions from an authoritative enrollment-process
  document.

### Non-Goals (v1)
- No rejection-with-reason flow: a step is simply **unverified** until stamped
  (explicit decision).
- No COR generation — external university system.
- No email notifications — in-app only.
- No SIMS API integration — officer checks SIMS manually, then stamps.
- No multi-campus/college scope — COT only.

## 3. Roles & Access

Four roles. Base role is **derived from the Google account email domain**;
role is fixed except where an Admin explicitly changes it.

| Role | Email domain | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Student** | `@student.buksu.edu.ph` | Complete steps 1–5, upload evidence, complete the step-3 Google Form, join department queue, see own progress, use chatbot. |
| **SBO Officer** | `@student.buksu.edu.ph` (promoted by Admin) | Verify/stamp steps 1–5, capture mugshots, review uploaded evidence, manage the step-3 Google Form link (set/edit/remove per academic year). |
| **Enrollment Adviser** | `@buksu.edu.ph` | Toggle queue availability for their department(s), process the FIFO queue, finalize enrollment. |
| **Admin** | any (bootstrapped) | Change student↔officer roles, manage departments, adviser↔department mapping, step-3 Google Form link, step-1 requirements list, academic years, chatbot knowledge doc. |

**Outside-organization accounts** (any other Google domain): allowed to sign
in, get the **Student** experience, and complete all steps, but every surface
shows a persistent notice banner ("You are outside the organization"). They can
still be promoted to officer by an Admin.

**Admin bootstrap:** first admin is seeded from an env list (e.g.,
`ADMIN_EMAILS`) and promoted on first sign-in. After that, Admins manage
promotions.

### Auth decisions
- Primary sign-in: **Google OAuth** (Better-Auth `google` plugin; creds already
  in `apps/web/.env`).
- Existing email/password auth: keep enabled for development/testing; decide at
  launch whether it ships to production.
- `auth.ts` currently has no plugins — add `google()`; role assignment happens
  in a post-sign-in hook (Better-Auth `after` callback / `user.created`).

## 4. The Six Steps

Cycle reset: **per academic year**. A new `AcademicYear` is activated by an
Admin; verifications from the previous year do not carry over.

| # | Step | Who acts | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Assessment** | Student applies → SBO officer | Student taps "Apply for assessment" (shows the requirements/payments list, managed by Admin). Officer checks SIMS manually, stamps verified when no balance remains. Unstamped = still pending (no rejection reason). |
| 2 | **Social media** | Student uploads → SBO officer | App lists the FB pages to like/follow (COT SBO, SSC, etc. — same list shown to everyone). Student uploads a **screenshot** as proof; officer opens/checks and stamps. |
| 3 | **Profiling** | Student submits via Google Form → SBO officer | **Google Form integration**. SBO Officers (and Admins) manage the active form link — one form per academic year — and can add, edit, or remove it. The form is configured to **collect the respondent's Google account email**. Student opens the form from the app, completes it, then uploads a **screenshot of the success page** ("Your response has been recorded") as proof. Officer cross-checks the Google Sheet response against the screenshot and stamps. |
| 4 | **Mugshot** | Officer captures **or** student uploads | Either: officer captures via office webcam (preferred) or student uploads a photo. Photo stored via UploadThing. Officer stamps. |
| 5 | **Parent consent + medical form** | Student uploads → SBO officer | Student uploads a scan/photo of the signed physical papers. Officer reviews the upload and stamps. |
| 6 | **Enrollment** | Student queues → Enrollment Adviser | See §5. Requires steps 1–5 all verified in the current academic year. |

**Step status model:** binary — `PENDING` (not yet stamped) or `VERIFIED`.
No rejection reasons in v1. The student dashboard shows the status of all six
steps plus, on step 1, the requirements/payments checklist.

## 5. Enrollment Queue (Step 6)

### Flow
1. **Prerequisite:** all steps 1–5 `VERIFIED` for the current `AcademicYear`.
2. Student opens step 6 → sees the **department list** (exact COT list TBD —
   user will supply; seed BSIT, BSEMC, BS Food Tech as examples) with each
   department's queue status (**Open** / **Closed**, and current position
   count).
3. Student **chooses a department** (not an adviser) and joins its queue →
   receives a position number + in-app notification.
4. Queue is **active only while at least one adviser of that department has
   availability toggled on**. When no adviser is open, joining is blocked
   ("Queue closed — adviser is not yet accepting students").
5. Adviser opens availability → sees their department's queue (FIFO by
   `joinedAt`) → **processes the next student** one at a time.
6. Adviser marks the student **enrolled** — one atomic action that (a) sets
   the queue entry to `COMPLETED` (`servedById`, `completedAt`) and (b) writes
   the step-6 **`StepVerification` as `VERIFIED`** (`stepId` = `ENROLLMENT`,
   `verifiedById` = adviser, `verifiedAt` = now) — the official enrollment
   record. (The adviser generates the COR in the separate university system in
   parallel.) The student sees a **confetti success screen** with a message:
   *"You are now enrolled in `<Department name>`, `<semester> <academic
   year>`"* (e.g., "1st Semester 2026-2027").
7. A completed enrollment sends an in-app notification and the queue position
   is cleared.

### Rules
- One queue entry per student per academic year (`@@unique`).
- Multiple advisers per department; one adviser may serve multiple departments
  (mapping managed by Admin).
- Availability is per adviser per department per academic year.
- Closing availability does not eject queued students — it stops new joins and
  pauses processing.
- `completeEnrollment` is **transactional**: the queue `COMPLETED` state and
  the step-6 `StepVerification` (`VERIFIED`) always land together — never one
  without the other. Completion is final (see §12).

## 6. Data Model (MongoDB + Prisma)

Conventions follow the existing repo: `String @id @map("_id")`, multi-file
schemas (`schema.prisma` for domain models, `auth.prisma` for auth models),
MongoDB relations use join collections where needed.

### Enums
```prisma
enum Role { STUDENT SBO_OFFICER ENROLLMENT_ADVISER ADMIN }
enum StepCode { ASSESSMENT SOCIAL_MEDIA PROFILING MUGSHOT CONSENT ENROLLMENT }
enum StepStatus { PENDING VERIFIED }
enum UploadKind { MUGSHOT SOCIAL_SCREENSHOT CONSENT_SCAN PROFILING_SCREENSHOT }
enum QueueStatus { WAITING IN_PROGRESS COMPLETED }
```

### Domain models (extend existing `auth.prisma` User)

**User — extend with:** `role Role @default(STUDENT)`, `isOutsideOrg Boolean
@default(false)` (email domain check at sign-in), `studentProfile
StudentProfile?`. (Student number is NOT on User — it lives on the profile;
see below.)

**Department**
- `id, name, code, isActive`
- Adviser mapping via join: **AdviserDepartment** `{ adviserId → User,
  departmentId → Department }`, `@@unique([adviserId, departmentId])`.

**AcademicYear**
- `id, label ("2026-2027"), semesterLabel ("1st Semester"), isActive`
- One active year at a time; gates all step verifications and queue joins.

**StudentProfile**
- `id, userId (unique → User), studentNumber (unique), academicYearId
  (first-registered year), createdAt`
- Student number is captured at **first login via a mini profile form**
  (name prefilled from Google).

**ProfilingForm** (step-3 Google Form config)
- `id, academicYearId (unique → AcademicYear), formUrl, updatedById → User?,
  updatedAt`
- One form per academic year, managed by SBO Officers and Admins (set/edit/
  remove). Answers live in Google Sheets — the app stores only the URL and the
  per-student submitted/verified state; no answer mirroring. No form entry →
  step 3 shows "form not yet set up" instead of the link.

**Step** (seeded constant data)
- `id, code StepCode, name, order` — six rows, created at seed.

**StepVerification** — the digital stamp
- `id, studentProfileId, stepId, academicYearId, status StepStatus
  @default(PENDING), appliedAt DateTime? (step 1), verifiedById → User?,
  verifiedAt DateTime?, evidence Json? (kind-specific payload, see below)`
- `@@unique([studentProfileId, stepId, academicYearId])`
- Evidence payloads: step 2 → `{ uploadIds: [Upload] }`; step 3 → `{
  uploadIds: [Upload] }` (success-page screenshot, `PROFILING_SCREENSHOT`);
  step 4 → `{ mugshotUploadId? }`; step 5 → `{ consentUploadId? }`; step 1 →
  `{}` (appliedAt is the record).
- `verifiedById`/`verifiedAt` provide the audit trail.
- Step 6 (`ENROLLMENT`) is verified by the **enrollment queue flow**, not the
  officer stamp flow: the adviser's `completeEnrollment` writes this record
  (`verifiedById` = adviser) atomically with the queue `COMPLETED` state (§5).
  Step 6 has no `appliedAt` — the queue join is the equivalent signal; steps
  1–5 are stamped by SBO officers.

**Upload**
- `id, fileKey, fileUrl, kind UploadKind, uploadedById → User, studentProfileId,
  createdAt` — records every UploadThing file (screenshot, mugshot, consent
  scan, profiling screenshot) so evidence is traceable.

**RequirementItem** (step-1 checklist shown to students)
- `id, label, order, isActive` — managed by Admin.

**EnrollmentQueue**
- `id, departmentId, studentProfileId, academicYearId, joinedAt, status
  QueueStatus @default(WAITING), servedById → User?, completedAt DateTime?`
- `@@unique([studentProfileId, academicYearId])`; position = order by
  `joinedAt`; index on `[departmentId, academicYearId, status]`.

**AdviserAvailability**
- `id, adviserId → User, departmentId, academicYearId, isOpen Boolean,
  openedAt DateTime?, closedAt DateTime?`
- `@@unique([adviserId, departmentId, academicYearId])`
- Department queue "open" state is **derived**: any adviser of that department
  with `isOpen = true`.

**Notification** (in-app only)
- `id, userId → User, type String, title, body, readAt DateTime?, createdAt`
- Events: step verified, queue joined, adviser opened/closed queue, enrollment
  completed.

**ChatbotKnowledge**
- `id, version Int, content String (markdown), updatedAt` — the
  enrollment-process document injected into the Groq prompt (user will supply
  the doc). Admin can edit/update.

## 7. API Surface (oRPC)

Follow existing `packages/api/src/routers/` pattern (`publicProcedure`,
`protectedProcedure`); add role-guarded procedures. Router sketch:

- **auth**: `signIn` (Google), `getSession`, `me` (includes `role`,
  `isOutsideOrg`, `studentProfile`).
- **profile**: `getMyProfile`, `upsertProfile` (student number mini-form at
  first login; server-side uniqueness check).
- **student**: `getMySteps` (6 steps + status + evidence + requirements list),
  `applyAssessment` (creates PENDING step-1 verification with `appliedAt`),
  `getProfilingForm` (active year's Google Form URL + the student's signed-in
  email as the submit-as hint), `uploadEvidence`
  (UploadThing server-side file router; steps 2/3/4/5), `getMyQueueStatus`.
- **officer** (role: SBO_OFFICER, ADMIN): `listPendingByStep` (filterable by
  step + year, paginated), `getStepDetail`, `verifyStep` (stamp: sets status
  VERIFIED + verifiedById/verifiedAt; guards "already verified"),
  `captureMugshot` (officer webcam capture → UploadThing), `upsertProfilingForm`
  (set/edit/remove the step-3 Google Form URL for the active academic year).
- **queue** (role: ENROLLMENT_ADVISER, ADMIN): `getDepartments`, `getQueueStatus`
  (per department: open/closed, count), `setAvailability`, `getMyQueues`
  (adviser's departments + FIFO lists), `processNextStudent`, `completeEnrollment`
  (transactionally: queue status `COMPLETED` + step-6 `StepVerification`
  `VERIFIED` with `verifiedById` + notification + success payload),
  `getEnrolledStudents`.
- **admin** (role: ADMIN): `listUsers`, `setRole` (student↔officer),
  departments CRUD, adviser↔department mapping, academic year CRUD (activate
  year), `listRequirementItems`/`upsertRequirementItem`, `updateChatbotKnowledge`,
  `upsertProfilingForm` (step-3 form link, shared with officers).
- **chatbot**: `ask` (public to authenticated users; Groq call — see §8).
- **notifications**: `listMine`, `markAllRead`.

All role checks enforced server-side (never trust the client); procedures
return only the caller's own data except where the role allows it.

## 8. Integrations

### Google Forms (step 3 — Profiling)
- **Link management:** SBO officers (and Admins) create the Google Form and
  paste its URL into the app — add, edit, or remove per academic year; one
  active form per year (`ProfilingForm`). No in-app form builder.
- **Identity:** the form is configured to **collect the respondent's Google
  account email**. The app shows the student's signed-in email next to the
  link so they submit as the same account; the officer matches sheet rows to
  students by email. Outside-org students submit the same way (form is not
  domain-restricted; matching is by email).
- **No response mirroring:** answers live only in the form's Google Sheet;
  the app stores the URL and per-student submitted/verified state.
- **Student proof:** screenshot of the success page ("Your response has been
  recorded") uploaded via UploadThing (`PROFILING_SCREENSHOT`); the officer
  cross-checks the sheet before stamping.

### Groq AI chatbot
- **Placement:** student page (and the same component usable on any page).
- **Purpose:** process FAQ assistant — steps, requirements, how things work.
- **No personal data access** — prompt contains only the knowledge document
  and the user's question.
- **Knowledge source:** `ChatbotKnowledge.content` (user will upload the
  enrollment-process doc later; stored as markdown). System prompt instructs
  the model to answer strictly from the doc and say when it doesn't know.
- **Env:** `GROQ_API_KEY`; model configurable (`GROQ_MODEL`, default
  `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`). Streaming responses preferred (Groq is fast;
  SvelteKit supports SSE).
- No chat-history persistence in v1 (stateless per request) — note as a later
  option.

### UploadThing
- **Files:** mugshot photo, social-media screenshot, profiling success-page
  screenshot, consent/medical scan —
  all images (`image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `image/webp`), max ~10 MB each.
- **Env:** `UPLOADTHING_TOKEN` (app id + secret in one token), SvelteKit
  adapter (`uploadthing/svelte`); server-side file router in the API package;
  file metadata stored on `Upload` model.
- Works on both serverless (Vercel) and Node servers (deployment undecided —
  see §11).

## 9. UI Surfaces (per role)

Design language: **`design.md` applies** — fluid springs, translucent
materials, inline validation, reduced-motion support, mobile-first student
experience (explicit requirement).

- **Login:** Google sign-in; outside-org users land in the student experience
  with the persistent notice banner.
- **First-login mini profile:** student number (required), name prefilled,
  then dashboard.
- **Student dashboard (mobile-first):** six-step progress checklist with
  status chips; step 1 shows requirements/payments list + "Apply for
  assessment" button; steps 2/3/4/5 show upload (or officer-capture) slots;
  step 3 additionally shows the Google Form link + signed-in email hint
  ("submit as <email>") and the success-page screenshot slot; step 6 shows
  department queue cards with open/closed status and join button; queue
  position shown after joining.
  Chatbot widget + notification bell.
- **SBO Officer dashboard:** pending-verification queue per step, student
  detail view (evidence, mugshot capture), stamp button;
  step-3 form link manager (set/edit/remove the active year's Google Form URL).
- **Enrollment Adviser dashboard:** their departments; availability toggles;
  FIFO queue with "process next"; enrollment completion with confetti success
  screen ("You are now enrolled in `<Department>`, `<semester> <year>`").
- **Admin dashboard:** role management (student↔officer table), departments,
  adviser↔department mapping, academic-year activation, requirements list
  editor, chatbot knowledge editor, step-3 form link (shared with officers).

## 10. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Security:** RBAC enforced server-side on every procedure; students can
  only read/write their own records (PII isolation — a student record action
  must never expose another student's data); uploads validated by UploadThing
  file router; audit trail via `verifiedById`/`verifiedAt`/`servedById`.
- **Performance:** step lists and queue views paginated; dashboard queries
  minimal (one aggregate read per surface); animations compositor-only per
  `design.md` §5.9.
- **Accessibility:** `design.md` §5.13 normative — `prefers-reduced-motion`,
  `-transparency`, `-contrast`; focus states; mobile touch targets.
- **Mobile:** student surfaces designed mobile-first (Tailwind responsive
  utilities); officer/adviser/admin primarily desktop.
- **Consistency:** follow existing monorepo conventions — oRPC routers in
  `packages/api`, Prisma in `packages/db`, env in `packages/env` (add
  `GROQ_API_KEY`, `GROQ_MODEL`, `UPLOADTHING_TOKEN`, `ADMIN_EMAILS`).

## 12. Edge Cases

- Student signs in with outside-org Google account → Student role + banner;
  can be promoted by Admin.
- Student applies for assessment with an outstanding balance → officer simply
  does not stamp; step stays PENDING. No rejection message in v1 (student sees
  the requirements list as the hint).
- Duplicate student number → server-side unique validation with inline error
  at the mini-profile step.
- Queue join attempt with steps 1–5 not all VERIFIED → blocked with a message
  listing the missing steps.
- Department queue closed (no adviser available) → join blocked; existing
  queued students remain and are served FIFO when an adviser reopens.
- Adviser closes availability mid-queue → no new joins; in-progress student
  finishes.
- Officer stamps an already-verified step → idempotent guard (no-op/error,
  timestamp preserved).
- Step-3 form link changed mid-year (officer edits/replaces it) → old
  submissions (success-page screenshots) remain attached to their
  StepVerification; new submissions use the new link. No answer snapshot
  exists in-app; per-year scoping comes from the per-year form.
- No form link set for the active year → step 3 shows "Form not yet set up —
  see SBO office" instead of the link; nothing to upload.
- Student resubmits the Google Form → officer stamps once; duplicate
  screenshot uploads don't alter an already-VERIFIED step (idempotent guard).
- Fake/edited success-page screenshot → officer cross-checks the Google Sheet
  for the student's email; mismatch means no stamp (stays PENDING, no
  rejection reason per §2).
- Academic year rollover → admin activates the new year; old verifications
  remain queryable but don't gate the new cycle; students redo steps.
- Same student, same year, re-joins queue after completion → blocked by
  `@@unique`; completion is final.
- Chatbot question outside the knowledge doc → model instructed to answer "I
  don't know / ask an SBO officer."
