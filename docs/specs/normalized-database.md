# ERPSYS database normalization: 1NF to 3NF

**Status:** Standalone design only. The [SQLite schema](normalized-database.sql) creates a fresh reference database; it does not change the app, migrate records, or authorize deleting MongoDB collections. SQLite 3.37+ makes the design executable locally without choosing the app's eventual SQL engine.

## Source and scope

The [final paper](../paper/FINAL_PAPER.md), especially §§1.2, 1.4–1.5, 2.2.1 (Tables 2.5–2.8), and 2.2.3, defines the business requirements. The [MongoDB registry](../../apps/server/src/lib/db/db.ts), [indexes](../../apps/server/src/lib/db/indexes.ts), [API documentation](../paper/API_DOCUMENTATION.md), and [collection storage notes](../ops/collection-simplification.md) show what is implemented. Where they differ, this design favors the written workflow. It covers student identity and yearly enrollment, five pre-enrollment reviews and completion, evidence, staff assignments and queues, schedules and handovers, notifications, administrative history, and approved assistant content. It does not model tuition payments, grades, class scheduling, or service credentials.

## How the schema reaches 3NF

The relation sketches below show the transformation, not intermediate schemas to deploy. `A → B` means that a value of `A` determines a value of `B`.

1. **Before normalization:** An enrollment-centric record might contain `student number, student name, period label, department name, year level, offered levels[], advisers[], submissions[{step, uploads[], answers[], decisions[{chips[]}]}], queue position, completed flag`. Flattening everything into one row per file and decision would multiply rows and make updates inconsistent.
2. **First normal form (1NF): One value per field.** Make one row per department level, submission attempt, evidence item, answer, review event, feedback chip, and requested transfer level. For example, `(submission_id, position) → file_id or source_url` and `(request_id, year_level)` identifies one requested level. A feedback note, answer, or approved guidance document is a single text value, not an encoded list of application records.
3. **Second normal form (2NF): Every non-key fact depends on the entire key.** In an atomic yearly worksheet keyed by `(student_profile_id, academic_year_id, step_code, attempt_no)`, the student number depends only on the profile; the semester label only on the period; and the department and class year on `(student_profile_id, academic_year_id)`. Move those facts to `student_profiles`, `academic_years`, and `student_enrollments`. The complete attempt key identifies a submission, not a second copy of the enrollment. Likewise, do not keep the file size on `(submission_id, position)`; it belongs to the referenced file. The DDL uses surrogate IDs where convenient and retains `UNIQUE` constraints on business keys.
4. **Third normal form (3NF): Remove facts determined through another non-key field.** The chains `enrollment_id → department_id → name and code`, `enrollment_id → student_profile_id → student_number`, and `submission_id → step_code → step name` are joined at read time. In particular, the queue references only `enrollment_id`, since its period and department come from the enrollment. Current review state, evidence counts, FIFO position and step 6 are derived, not stored as competing facts. Historical names, feedback, and stamp references describe a particular event, not what the user or form looks like today; these snapshots are intentional.

## Final relations and collection decisions

- **Identity and school structure (7):** `users` keeps only the business identity and role (no password, MFA secret, session, or OAuth data); `student_profiles` holds the student number without a redundant current-year pointer; `student_enrollments` holds one departmental record per student and academic year/semester. `academic_years` allows at most one active period. `profiling_forms` is an optional one-to-one child for a year's Google Form link and its own lifecycle metadata; absence means unlinked. `departments` owns its name and independently editable code. `department_year_levels` replaces both `department.yearLevels[]` and `adviser_department.yearLevels[]`: one row per department and level with zero or one assigned adviser. Keep withdrawn offerings inactive rather than removing rows referenced by past enrollments. Expand old “all levels” values into explicit rows when evaluating a future migration.
- **Workflow and evidence (9):** `workflow_steps` seeds the six step definitions. `step_submissions` keeps successive attempts; `submission_events` records submission, approval, return, and withdrawal. One pending assessment can be submitted without a file; other evidence is linked as required by the workflow. `stored_files` has one canonical private object key for each retained file; `submission_evidence` links a file or permitted source URL, `submission_answers` holds one captured answer per row, and `submission_feedback_chips` keeps one feedback item per row. `assessment_requirements` and `social_media_links` are separate editable catalogs, not alternative names for workflow steps. Unlike the current app's current-profile stamp display, approvals can retain the name and stamp file references used at the time. Keep files private, check permissions before serving them, and apply the approved retention rules.
- **Advisers and queues (6):** `adviser_availability` stores live opening and heartbeat state; `office_hour_entries` stores weekly hours, one-off hours and date cancellations in Asia/Manila. They are not duplicates. `department_transfer_requests` and `department_transfer_levels` preserve handover status, expiry and requested scope, even after responsibility changes. `queue_entries` has at most one ticket per yearly enrollment; `enrollment_events` preserves each completion or reversal. There is no separate step-6 review or authoritative “completed” queue status. A removed ticket keeps its original `joined_at` and a deadline for Undo; ordinary rejoin or requeue can assign a new time. Share links can remain signed, expiring tokens; their creation does not need a table. `waiting_queue` shows the overall departmental FIFO position, with ties broken by ticket ID; service still selects the oldest student eligible for that adviser's year-level scope.
- **Communication and administration (3):** `notifications` includes the paper's read indicator as a single nullable `read_at` on the recipient's notification; the current app writes read markers locally. This reference design makes the marker durable without a separate receipts table. `audit_events` records important administrative actions, separately from generic HTTP telemetry. `assistant_guidance` is one current, revisioned office-approved document; it excludes chat transcripts and quota counters.
- **Do not persist twice:** Reports, PDFs, dashboard totals, step progress, evidence counts, and queue rank are queries or exports. Department codes are authored in the reference design because the paper calls for managing names and codes; the current app derives codes from names. The yearly form link is optional, but there is no second independent source for it. The registry's `adminActionLog` and `auditLog` already name the same collection; distinct action and HTTP events within it are not duplicate data.

## Supporting storage not modeled as business tables

- **Better Auth:** Reuse the auth identity when integrating; `users.id` represents that identity in the standalone SQL reference, not a second sign-in account. Keep `user` auth-only fields and the `session`, `account`, `verification`, `twoFactor`, and `admin_step_up` data under auth ownership. Password assistance, lockout session revocation, and authenticator reset use that subsystem.
- **Integration and storage:** `integration` (credential overrides, versions, and switches), `calendar_connection` (OAuth secrets and sync retries), R2 bytes, and pending upload sessions remain outside this schema. `stored_files` tracks retained objects, not temporary sessions or URLs with access tokens. Keep `chat_usage`, `storage_usage`, `platform_state` (including TTL rate limits and timestamp allocation), and HTTP request audit data as operational state where needed. The older `admin_action_log`, `integration_setting`, `integration_credential` and `profiling_form` names identify historical MongoDB collections, not new SQL aliases or permission to delete data.

## Constraints, timestamps, and boundary of the reference design

Every base table has non-null `created_at` and `updated_at` with UTC defaults. Writers supply canonical UTC timestamps for events and set `updated_at` on mutable writes; the schema's completion and reversal triggers update the ticket timestamp themselves. Submission, completion, and administrative event rows are intended to be append-only through the service, apart from controlled actor anonymization or retention. Event-sequence views treat increasing integer IDs as append order; use generated IDs for normal writes. Database checks cover keys and referential integrity, review event order, five verified steps before join/service/completion, at most one student served per adviser and per department/period, and valid completion/reversal pairs. Reversal requeues if the five approvals still hold; otherwise the student needs to regain eligibility before rejoining. Department and level cannot be rewritten while queued or once completion history exists, so later edits do not reinterpret earlier completion records.

A future application must enforce role and account status, active-period and offered-level eligibility, live availability with heartbeat expiry, FIFO within adviser scope, attachment ownership and post-submission immutability, assessment and evidence rules, transfer and Undo timing, schedule conflicts, notification dispatch, cross-step effects of withdrawn approvals, and access control and retention. These need transaction-level checks and are not all expressible as keys or row checks. Account deletion must respect references and retention; the reference schema does not perform production account cleanup. Current MongoDB timestamp ordering and API behavior are unchanged.
